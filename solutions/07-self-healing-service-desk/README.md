# Self-Healing Service Desk

**Status: Designed. Not built yet.**

Tickets resolved, and every resolution becomes knowledge.

## What it is

A ticket swarm of triage, resolution, change-risk and knowledge-writing agents, working in the languages of all sites. Every resolved ticket becomes a knowledge article automatically.

## The management issue it solves

Slow support, repeated incidents and a high cost per ticket.

## Why it matters to the business

Employees get an answer in minutes in their own language, and the same problem is never solved twice. The metrics are simple to audit: deflection rate and time to resolution.

## Agents

- Triage agent
- Resolution agent
- Change-risk agent
- Knowledge-writing agent

## Data needed

All data is synthetic and belongs to the fictional company described in [docs/company-profile.md](../../docs/company-profile.md).

- Synthetic ticket history in several languages
- Knowledge base articles
- Configuration and change records

## Additional features planned

- Onboarding and offboarding automation
- Plant-floor IT support by voice
