# Incident INC-001 at Halden Plant 04 (fictional): picture for the crisis team

Generated by `python -m sentinel run`. Synthetic data, simulated tools, fixed rules, no language model. Every euro figure is calculated from the assumptions listed at the end; none is a measured loss.

## In short

An intruder moved from the office network to the plant floor between 02:10 and 03:02 UTC and now controls 3 devices. 3 production lines are within reach. The recommended response costs EUR 120,000 in lost output and leaves no line within reach. Disconnecting the widest area instead would cost EUR 465,500. Nothing has been disconnected: the request is waiting for the Head of OT Security.

## Assumptions behind the figures

- Cost of one stopped line-hour: EUR 15,000 (company profile).
- Assumption: a line keeps running this long on orders already loaded when the link to the office side is closed. Value used: 6 hours.
- Assumption: time to restore a line whose controllers and workstations were encrypted. Value used: 48 hours.
- Assumption: a disconnected cell stops its whole line. Value used: yes.
- Disconnect Line B, cell 2: held for 8 hours. Assumption: time to check and clean the devices of one cell.
- Close the link between office side and plant floor: held for 4 hours. Assumption: time to clean the remote access gateway and replace the stolen account.
- Disconnect the whole plant floor: held for 8 hours. Assumption: time to check and clean the devices of the plant floor.
- Line A (casting): 12 further hours after any stop. Assumption, from the author's plant experience: a casting line cannot be stopped and restarted like other lines. An unplanned stop costs this many further hours before good output returns.
- Lost output is counted only for hours in which the schedule says the line would have run.
