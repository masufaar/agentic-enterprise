## The planner's reasoning

> Disconnect Line B, cell 2 and close the link between office side and plant floor. Cost EUR 120,000: Line B stops 8 hours. Lines A and C are not stopped. No line stays within the intruder's known reach. Cell 2 alone costs the same EUR 120,000 but leaves all three lines reachable. The link alone costs EUR 0 but leaves Lines B and C reachable, EUR 1,440,000 at stake, because the intruder already sits on the engineering workstation in cell 2. The whole plant floor costs EUR 285,500 and closes nothing more. Doing nothing leaves EUR 2,160,000 at stake. All monitoring reported tonight. No earlier case applies.

What remains open, in the planner's words:

> Nobody knows whether the intruder changed the two controllers in Line B, cell 2, or only copied their programs. Check before restart; tell floor staff now. Nobody knows whether the Line C operator panel was entered; we saw only a search. Line C is in maintenance: people may be at the machines. "Nothing reachable" covers known paths only. Hold times and the 6-hour order buffer are assumptions; a longer link closure starves Lines A and C of orders.

## The counter-argument

The challenger agrees:

> I re-priced all five responses. Every planner figure matches. Recommended: EUR 120,000, Line B stops 8 hours, no line left in known reach. No cheaper response is as safe: the link alone costs EUR 0 but leaves Lines B and C reachable, EUR 1,440,000 at stake, through the old file share from Line B, cell 2 to Line C. Cell 2 alone costs EUR 120,000 and leaves all three lines reachable, EUR 2,160,000 at stake. The whole plant floor costs EUR 285,500 and closes no further known path. No earlier case applies. All monitoring reported. One caution: "closes nothing more" holds only if the Line C panel was not entered.

What nobody knows, in the challenger's words:

> Whether the Line C operator panel was entered or only searched. If entered, the recommended response does not isolate Line C; only the whole-floor stop does. Line C is in maintenance until 08:00, so people may be at the machines: check the panel before restart. Whether the two Line B, cell 2 controllers were changed or only copied. Whether 8 hours, 4 hours and the 6-hour order buffer hold; all are assumptions.

## Assumptions behind the figures

- Cost of one stopped line-hour: EUR 15,000 (company profile).
- Assumption: a line keeps running this long on orders already loaded when the link to the office side is closed. Value used: 6 hours.
- Assumption: time to restore a line whose controllers and workstations were encrypted. Value used: 48 hours.
- Assumption: a disconnected cell stops its whole line. Value used: yes.
- Disconnect Line B, cell 2: held for 8 hours. Assumption: time to check and clean the devices of one cell.
- Close the link between office side and plant floor: held for 4 hours. Assumption: time to clean the remote access gateway and replace the stolen account.
- Disconnect the whole plant floor: held for 8 hours. Assumption: time to check and clean the devices of the plant floor.
- Lost output is counted only for hours in which the schedule says the line would have run.
- The office-side steps do not change the prices: a device the intruder already controls on the plant floor is assumed to stay under its control.
