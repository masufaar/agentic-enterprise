# Business case: AI Value Ledger

**Company:** Halden Industrial (fictional). **All figures are illustrative assumptions, not measured results.** Replace them with your own numbers.

## The problem

The company spends on AI and cannot say what it gets back.

## The proposal

A cost-and-benefit account for every agent in this repository: running cost, hours saved, cash impact and risk reduced, rolled up into a board dashboard.

## Value levers

| Lever | Assumption | Illustrative value |
|---|---|---|
| AI budget redeployed to higher-value use | EUR 3M yearly AI spend x 10% | EUR 300,000 |

Further benefits, not quantified here:

- A factual answer to 'what do we get for our AI spend?'
- Risk and return per agent on one screen

## Cost

- **Build effort:** 40 to 60 hours for a thin working slice (estimate for one person working with an AI coding assistant).
- **Running cost:** Small model for collection and reporting; most of the ledger is ordinary data processing.

## Controls

Every agent in this solution is registered in the [AI Governance Control Tower](../01-ai-governance-control-tower/README.md), acts within written rules and is logged. Actions with high impact wait for a named person.

## Decision asked of the board

Require a cost and benefit line for every AI agent in the company.
